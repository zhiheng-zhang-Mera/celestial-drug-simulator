Raw response hashes in that fixture are historical metadata, not a reproducible raw-file archive.

The model uses DailyMed STRATTERA section12.3: https://dailymed.nlm.nih.gov/dailymed/drugInfo.cfm?setid=b080e703-3fab-4914-8529-ffaa59da693d . CL/F group coefficient is0.35L/h/kg for normal and0.03 for poor; half-lives5.2/21.6h. CL/F=coefficient×weight; AUC per dose=dose/(CL/F). F must not be applied again. These are conditional group-parameter estimates, not validated individual exposures. Legacy extensive-metabolizer to modern normal mapping is an explicit research approximation. No IM/UM interpolation, copy-number inference, absorption/Cmax curve, PD, dosing recommendation or DDI multiplication.

The user explicitly confirms a normal-organ, nonpregnant/nonlactating, no-other-exposure research scenario. Implementation bounds18–64years,40–120kg,>0–100mg are conservative operational limits, not validated clinical eligibility or dosage advice. Genotype-predicted phenotype is separate from measured enzyme activity. Height/fat/sex are not numerical modifiers. CPIC gene-drug presence is not quantitative model validation. Clinical review, calibration, prediction intervals, mixture resolution and actual multi-drug metabolic adjustment remain pending.
